Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

n8n Job Radar (“the app”) is a personal automation tool owned and used only by the owner of this website, Ahmed Aly Ghaly. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner is expected to sign in to it.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to the owner’s own Google account, and only to:

  • Gmail — to read job-alert emails that arrive in the owner’s inbox.
  • Google Sheets — to record the results in a spreadsheet owned by the owner.

How data is used and shared

Data from these services is used only for that purpose. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and it is not stored anywhere other than the owner’s Google account and the owner’s own device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading any further data. Spreadsheet rows the app has written can be deleted directly from the owner’s Google Sheet.

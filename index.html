Looking good is the last decision, not the first.

Four stages, each with one rule I keep coming back to.

Think

Position before pieces.

I start with who's using it, what they're trying to get done, and what a wrong turn costs them. Screens come later.

Design

Roles before components.

Every element gets one job. Hierarchy, flows, and variants are decided as a system, not screen by screen.

Build

The design ships as designed.

Components, tokens, real breakpoints, fast pages. What was decided in Figma survives contact with the browser.

Refine

Play the odds, then check the details.

Launch, watch what people actually do, adjust. Then the 8px.
